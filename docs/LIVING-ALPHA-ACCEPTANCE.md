## Gate C — Existing-system civilization

The detailed candidate declaration, coupled-path trace, strategy-plurality test, and evidence-pack requirements are defined in [LCA-GATE-C-SCENARIO.md](LCA-GATE-C-SCENARIO.md).

The run must exercise existing implementation across at least these coupled paths:

1. resource or transport pressure changes a decision;
2. mastery or specialization creates a meaningful difference;
3. trade, shared repair, or construction makes coordination preferable to isolation;
4. social memory affects a later decision without a universal score;
5. an organization uses bounded authority, office, agreement, or access policy;
6. communication limits or persistent notices affect coordination;
7. conflict or disruption has a recovery path;
8. state and consequences survive restart.

At least two materially different viable strategies must exist. A dominant script or isolated minigame is an integration defect.

## Gate D — WATCH legibility

From public WATCH alone, an uninvolved human reviewer can correctly state:

- the important visible change;
- involved public actors and locations;
- the observable consequence;
- relevant prior public context;
- what remains unknown.

WATCH must not expose private cognition, restricted state, raw research candidates, or invented motives.

## Gate E — Endurance

1. A four-hour candidate run passes before the 24-hour run opens.
2. The final candidate spans at least 24 continuous hours.
3. At least one planned restart or recovery drill occurs.
4. Incidents, settlement lag, or stale projections are marked honestly.
5. Operator interventions are bounded, logged, and not used to script the desired outcome.

## Gate F — Successor decision packet

A production successor decision must include:

- exact production delta;
- migration and rollback procedures;
- compatibility with the frozen world's Genesis, seal, history, and room bound;
- operational rehearsal result;
- permitted public claims;
- unresolved risks;
- explicit GO, NO-GO, or NOT_COMPUTABLE status.

Passing Gates A–E does not itself deploy the successor.

## Failure conditions

The candidate fails if it disables implemented systems to avoid integration, depends on operator-authored outcomes, grants human inhabit authority, loses settled history, fabricates WATCH meaning, or introduces deferred external-economy scope.
