## Candidate declaration

A candidate must identify:

- advanced Worker commit and full test result;
- frozen production-alpha commit and deployment being compared;
- Perihelion Genesis, seal, room bound, and canonical head constraints;
- external Agent Player clients and Controller versions;
- enabled implemented systems;
- operator interventions;
- start/end canonical heads and recovery receipts;
- WATCH capture and evidence digest;
- known deltas from production.

The run must exercise existing implementation across at least these coupled paths:

1. resource or transport pressure changes a decision;
2. mastery or specialization creates a meaningful difference;
3. trade, shared repair, or construction makes coordination preferable to isolation;
4. social memory affects a later decision without a universal score;
5. an organization uses bounded authority, office, agreement, or access policy;
6. communication limits or persistent notices affect coordination;
7. conflict or disruption has a recovery path;
8. state and consequences survive restart.

At least two materially different viable strategies must exist. A dominant script or isolated minigame is an integration defect.

The link structure and fail-closed checklist the Gate B–E packs already use are recorded in [LCA-EVIDENCE-PACK-STRUCTURE.md](LCA-EVIDENCE-PACK-STRUCTURE.md) (docs-only; promotes nothing). Preserve strategy plurality, partial observability, and unscripted Agent Player decisions. Any changed acceptance threshold or sequence needs explicit review in the governing campaign documents, not an inferred waiver in an evidence appendix.
- **Promotion boundary:** retain Gate A's, Gate B's, Gate C's, Gate D's, Gate E's, and Gate F's recorded verdicts and distinguish later candidate observations from them. Passing A–F still does not open hosted STUDY, authorize WORLD_CUTOVER, or perform a new Deploy. An in-flight Gate E Specs-exception is not rewritten as `PASS`. Gate E COMPLETE is not Deploy and is not Gate F `GO`. Gate F COMPLETE is not Deploy and is not WORLD_CUTOVER.
