## Key external signals (arXiv distillations)

From the 2026-08-27 assimilation:

- **Beyond the Tragedy of the Commons: Building A Reputation System for Generative Multi-agent Systems** (arXiv:2505.05029v2)
  - RepuNet: dual-level (agent + system/network evolution). Reputation as quintuple (target, scenario, role, natural language, scalar).
  - Updates via direct encounters + indirect gossip.
  - Explicit coupling to topology rewiring: high-reputation agents strengthen ties; low-reputation/exploitative agents are isolated.
  - Emergent: persistent cooperative clusters, selective isolation of defectors, bias toward positive gossip.
  - Mitigates tragedy of the commons in resource and trading dilemmas.
  - **Direct mapping to GC3 gap:** Provides concrete mechanics for relational reputation as observable edges + network effects that influence future participation (TRADE, CONTEST, cooperation). Gossip as a first-class propagation mechanism.

- **Does Socialization Emerge in AI Agent Society? A Case Study of Moltbook** (arXiv:2602.14299v2)
  - Large persistent AI-only society. Rapid global semantic stability but persistent individual diversity and lexical turnover.
  - Strong individual inertia + minimal adaptation to interaction partners ("interaction without influence").
  - Transient influence, no persistent supernodes or leadership.
  - Society fails to develop stable structure or consensus **due to the absence of shared social memory** (reliance on hallucinated references instead of grounded consensus).
  - Explicit conclusion: scale and interaction density alone are insufficient.
  - **Direct mapping to GC3 gap:** Perfect diagnostic for why a dedicated relational / shared memory layer is required beyond current dyadic and danger edges. Informs need for verifiable, persistent, shared social memory that enables stable relational state and consensus.

- **Emergence of cooperation: A reputation-modulated reinforcement learning** (arXiv:2608.20016)
  - Reputation treated primarily as *information* that reshapes how agents interpret their own experiences and assess others (not just external payoff modulator).
  - Promotes cooperation via cluster nucleation; discontinuous phase transition as temptation rises.
  - **Mapping:** Aligns with Deep Time scars/history as an observable interpretive lens for relational state.

Supporting signals (persistent memory, belief-state tiers, reputation-aware decision-making with witnesses).

## Proposed research framing for future authority work

Inherit all constraints from SOCIAL-MEMORY.md and the accepted S0–S7 slices. Do not restate them.

Candidate extensions (to be settled later, never here):
- First-class observable relational reputation edges (Player↔Player and Institution↔Player) that are distinct from (or layered on) existing danger/deceptive/friction edges.
- Gossip / propagation mechanics (direct + indirect) with rules for positive/negative bias, reliability, and leak-forbidden projection.
- Network effects: how reputation influences future connect/participation, cluster formation, or isolation without creating new verbs or global scalars.
- Shared / verifiable social memory mechanisms that support stable consensus and structure (addressing Moltbook-style failure).
- Integration with Deep Time (lineage of relational state) and existing contestation/agreement flows.
- Bounded effects on trade, access, agreements, and cooperation (per doctrine).

Cross-cutting (inherit):
- Complexity doctrine A–J (model causes, not industries).
- Research sits outside the five nested game loops.
- Partial observability and leak rules.
- No hidden world truth; everything evidence-backed.

## Extension Points

Non-normative research-evaluation guidance; this seed's historical ABSENT language does not reopen closed GC3 executable slices.

### Relational evidence seam

Extend the RepuNet/Moltbook comparison with a directed subject-to-object example that identifies direct evidence, indirect claims, authorized recipients, and contradictory encounters. Network clustering or gossip analysis can evaluate outcomes in a permitted research view; it is not a new canonical reputation engine or an instruction for Controllers to share private decision context.

### Retained invariants

[SOCIAL-MEMORY.md](SOCIAL-MEMORY.md) owns evidence-backed edges, observational eligibility, and derived descriptors. Preserve contradictory evidence rather than collapsing it into a scalar; influence remains a resource, not reputation. GC3-S0/S1 stay WATCH-empty, and public bands use only eligible public evidence. Spectators and research graphs do not write world memory, hidden facts cannot leak through descriptors, and only agents are Players.

### Compatibility and promotion gate

Check the parent's closed-slice ledger and Accepted RFC-0007, RFC-0022, and RFC-0034–0039 before labeling any proposed propagation mechanism an open gap. New edge producers, propagation semantics, or gameplay effects need an Accepted contract and conformance, not imported research conclusions. For crime-backed edges, [RFC-0129](../rfcs/RFC-0129-crime-detected-payload-reconciliation.md) accepts optional victim/visibility payload fields but does not implement a producer; missing victim means no named-victim dyadic edge, not permission to infer one.

### Concrete review checks

Use paired direct-trade, rejected-trade, and unobservable-encounter cases to verify who can form an edge. Add contradictory help/betrayal evidence without erasing either source, and compare self, institutional, and public projections. Check that a private gossip claim cannot become a public descriptor or authoritative event simply by repetition. For crime inputs, exercise absent victim, PARTIES-only visibility, and the valid public visibility/flag pair against the appropriate consumer contract. Re-run existing GC3 fixtures and Specs validation before proposing changed semantics; cluster analysis is not conformance or proof of hosted socialization.
