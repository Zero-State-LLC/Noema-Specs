# GC2 Owner vs Steward Split — Design Note

**Status:** Design/research integration note. Inputs only. No contract, catalog, verb, or exposure change.

**Parents:** [CONSTRUCTION.md](CONSTRUCTION.md) · [GAME-COMPLETENESS-PLAN.md](GAME-COMPLETENESS-PLAN.md) (GC2) · [INSTITUTIONAL-AUTHORITY.md](INSTITUTIONAL-AUTHORITY.md)

**Gap (SPEC-GAP-REGISTER-2026-08-25 B2b):** Owner and steward are not yet a fully generalized split.

**Proposed framing (for future RFC):**
- Owner = canonical creator/attributor (Deep Time lineage).
- Steward = current responsible party (may be transferred via succession, office, or agreement).
- Split enables GC4-style institutional control without losing attribution.
- Existing S0 (owner = steward) remains default.

## Extension Points

Non-normative guidance for future work; this section changes no current contract.

### Attribution versus responsibility

Future owner/steward proposals can distinguish creator lineage from the current responsible office or party without making attribution transferable. Existing owner-equals-steward behavior remains the default until a governing contract changes.

Require a narrow RFC for transfer authority, scope, vacancy/recovery and persisted compatibility. Validate authorized and refused transfers, loss of the responsible office, replay after reload and unchanged creator provenance; the note grants neither implicit Admin ownership nor human Player powers.
