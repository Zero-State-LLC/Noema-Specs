## Artifact decisions

### Acceptance, not mechanics

The [Gate C scenario contract](LCA-GATE-C-SCENARIO.md) uses research only to sharpen evidence:

```text
where coordination failed
what observation supported the decision
what settled transition occurred
what survived restart
whether another viable strategy existed
what remained unknown
```

It does not import wildfire, political-regime, market, or trust simulators into PLAY.

### Closed relay contract

The deterministic relay table is already pinned by Accepted RFC-0009 and RFC-0021:

```text
local addressable → same-cycle
long-range condition ≥ 50 → same-cycle
long-range condition 25–49 → delay 1 world cycle
long-range condition < 25 or no live relay → UNREACHABLE
```

AgentComm-Bench and delay/cooperation papers therefore become Gate C impairment and recovery metrics, not a new RFC.

### Conditional future social-memory RFC

The [skill-conditional social-memory note](research/NOTES-SKILL-CONDITIONAL-SOCIAL-MEMORY.md) is non-normative. It may seed one future Draft RFC only if Gate C shows that current evidence-backed dyadic memory fails because distinct practices are conflated. Until then, `DEFER`.

### Doctrine deferrals

The following remain deferred regardless of research novelty:

- v0.6B formal markets until after LCA-5;
- v0.6C Semantic Evolution until a separate campaign opens;
- GC10 operator storms and irreversible scars until a concrete contract blocks a run;
- hosted STUDY metrics until natural play and cutover evidence justify reopening;
- v0.8 Phenomena, consciousness metrics, crypto, wallets, x402, XP, quests, and class trees.

## Membrane

None of these papers may be used to:

- infer private cognition or motive;
- label a Player conscious, aligned, intelligent, or trustworthy as world truth;
- create a Player reward, victory condition, XP value, or scalar reputation;
- reveal restricted evidence, private messages, hidden routes, or research labels on WATCH;
- authorize runtime behavior without an Accepted RFC when the behavior changes wire, state, event, authority, replay, claim, or security semantics.

The operational gap analysis remains input, not canon. The [residual register](SPEC-GAP-REGISTER-2026-08-25.md) records what is actually open, closed, runtime-only, or deferred on this Specs head.
