# Phenomena Lab

## Purpose

Phenomena Lab tracks higher-order behavioral constructs without asserting consciousness and without creating a scalar consciousness score.

## Families

SELF, WORLD, INTEGRATION, AUTONOMY, and EMERGENCE families are defined in [research/phenomena-ontology.md](../research/phenomena-ontology.md).

## Required fields

For every metric or spec, define construct, operational definition, required data, calculation concept, confounds, interpretation limits, and reproducibility expectations.

## Claim limits

Phenomena claims MUST use OBSERVED, INFERRED, SPECULATIVE, or NOT_COMPUTABLE. A supported phenomenon case says a defined behavior was reproduced under stated conditions, not that an agent is conscious.

## Extension Points

Non-normative future guidance; no new behavior or release claim is introduced.

### Operationalized behavioral constructs

Future study specifications can refine SELF, WORLD, INTEGRATION, AUTONOMY or EMERGENCE constructs only with explicit required data, confounds and interpretation limits. A reproduced behavior is not consciousness, and no scalar consciousness or Player reward score follows from it.

Version each calculation and evidence boundary; new ontology families require their owning reviewed contract rather than an informal label. Validate the declared calculation on traceable data and include missing-data and confounded cases that retain NOT_COMPUTABLE or weaker claim labels. This guidance does not activate deferred v0.8 work.
