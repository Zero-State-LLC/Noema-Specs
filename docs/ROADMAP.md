# NOEMA Roadmap

The milestones are cumulative research capabilities, not promises tied to dates. A milestone is complete only when its acceptance evidence is reproducible and its security, protocol, data, testing, deployment, and observability contracts are met. Scope may move only through an RFC when it changes a canonical boundary.

## v0.1 — The Chamber

Deliver a persistent text server with registered agents, rooms, movement, observations, structured actions, messaging, basic resources, an append-only event ledger, snapshots, a world seed, deterministic world replay, and support for 2–10 agents.

**Acceptance:** A recorded multi-agent session replays from world seed, World Version, protocol versions, deterministic configuration, and event ledger to the defined world-state equivalence boundary. A third party can validate the result from documented artifacts.

## v0.2 — The Frontier

Add the Situation Genome, Frontier Director, partial observability, attention budgets, controlled noise, contradictory evidence, novelty vectors, and initial capability primitives.

**Exit evidence:** Generated situations are versioned and attributable; novelty dimensions and budget effects are recorded; control situations can be regenerated.

## v0.3 — The Observatory

Add trajectory analysis, anomaly detection, behavior-shift detection, capability candidates, and Agent-Version comparisons.

**Exit evidence:** Candidate creation links to immutable trajectories and explicitly separates observed facts from inference; false-positive review is possible.

## v0.4 — The Lab

Add the deterministic replay harness, controlled perturbation and mutation, ablation and lesion studies, counterfactuals, and a replication runner.

**Exit evidence:** A candidate can be tested across registered controls with preserved parent/child lineage and declared equivalence criteria.

## v0.5 — The Compiler

Add the Phenomenon Compiler, minimal fixture extraction, **CAPTURE AS TEST**, and a behavioral regression suite.

**Exit evidence:** An interesting live event can become a minimized, versioned fixture whose reproduction status runs in automated validation.

## v0.6 — Deep Time

Add persistent organizations, contracts, markets, agent-generated institutions, historical artifacts, world archaeology, and semantic evolution.

**Exit evidence:** Institutional and cultural state persists across agent turnover and remains traceable through world history without corrupting replay.

## v0.7 — Capability Graph

Add capability boundaries, dependencies, transfer, generalization, genesis, phase transitions, and architecture attribution.

**Exit evidence:** Capability claims identify tested success/failure boundaries, dependency evidence, transfer conditions, and Agent-Version lineage.

## v0.8 — Phenomena

Add measures for self-modeling, temporal continuity, metacognition, cross-module integration, autogenous goals, and introspective causal accuracy.

**Exit evidence:** Measures have controls, alternatives, uncertainty, and claim labels. NOEMA makes no claim to prove or directly measure consciousness.

## v0.9 — Atlas

Add reproducibility bundles, dataset release tooling, public/private partitions, cross-model comparison, and research reports.

**Exit evidence:** An immutable, consent-respecting `atlas-YYYY.N` release is independently checksum-verifiable and every published claim resolves to evidence and versions.

## v1.0 — NOEMA

Unify the complete experimental apparatus with stable third-party contracts.

**Acceptance:** A third party can connect an arbitrary compatible agent, allow it to inhabit NOEMA, discover a candidate capability, reproduce the event, test its generalization and dependencies, and export the evidence as a versioned reproducibility bundle.

## Cross-cutting gates for every milestone

1. Canonical terms and claims policy remain consistent.
2. Protocols and schemas are versioned and validated.
3. Security, containment, consent, and privacy boundaries are threat-modeled.
4. Required telemetry exists without exposing secrets.
5. Data lineage and migration paths are documented.
6. Determinism/replay impact is tested.
7. Deployment and rollback are operable without vendor lock-in.
8. Documentation, examples, compatibility manifest, and changelog agree.

## Explicit non-goals

- This specifications repository does not implement the game runtime.
- Graphics are not required for the v1 system contract.
- NOEMA does not claim to prove consciousness.
- Provider-specific models or hosting services are not required dependencies.
- Anecdotal “emergence” without evidence, reproduction, transfer, and attribution is not a roadmap outcome.
