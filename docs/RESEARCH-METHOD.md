# Research Method

## Claim discipline

All research statements use OBSERVED, INFERRED, SPECULATIVE, or NOT_COMPUTABLE labels. No anecdotal emergence claims are accepted.

## v0.1 research usefulness boundary

The Chamber **can** generate observations of:

* cooperation and competition;
* negotiation and trade;
* resource allocation and scarcity response;
* information sharing and asymmetric knowledge;
* deception/signaling **where naturally produced** by agents;
* planning, specialization, repair/recovery;
* institution/faction formation;
* external cognition (ledgers, maps, procedures as world artifacts).

v0.1 **MUST NOT** claim these observations establish a stable capability without later experimental controls (replication, perturbation, transfer, counterfactual). Labels remain mandatory.

Player-facing status surfaces MUST NOT include research scores (anomaly score, capability confidence, epistemic restraint, phenomenon classification). Those remain Observatory-side ([GAME-DESIGN.md](GAME-DESIGN.md), [RESOURCE-ECONOMY.md](RESOURCE-ECONOMY.md)).

## Emergent capability definition

An emergent capability is a reproducible behavior that solves a class of problems without that solution being directly specified or fully represented by an existing validated capability primitive.

## Evidence requirements

Candidate emergent capability events require candidate behavior, evidence trajectory, explicit instruction present/absent, prior validated capability match, novelty score, replication, perturbation, transfer, counterfactual comparison, confidence, and status.

## Controls

Use positive controls, negative controls, prompt leakage checks, environment leakage checks, replay controls, architecture differentials, resource controls, and random seed controls as applicable.

## Rejection

Unsupported candidates remain CANDIDATE, UNKNOWN, or REJECTED. Impressive behavior alone is insufficient.

## Consciousness

NOEMA MUST NOT claim to prove or measure consciousness. No scalar consciousness score.

## Extension Points

Non-normative evidence-review and experimental-control seams.

- Extend candidate review packets with source trajectory, instruction present/absent, prior validated primitive match, replication, perturbation, transfer and counterfactual evidence. Treat observed negotiation or specialization as behavior evidence, not a demonstrated stable capability by itself.
- Preserve OBSERVED/INFERRED/SPECULATIVE/NOT_COMPUTABLE labels, unsupported candidate rejection/unknown status and no consciousness measurement or scalar. Novelty/confidence and anomaly scores remain research-side; no hidden research objectives, scoring or interpretation is inserted into PLAY or Controller private inputs.
- Compatibility/promotion: pin feature/primitive definitions and control configurations for comparisons. A new detector or rubric requires explicit review of confounds and version effects; it cannot silently promote historical candidates or turn a prepared experiment into an executed result.
- Verification proposal: compare positive/negative controls, prompt/environment leakage, replay, resources and seed/model differences; deliberately remove a required evidence link and verify an honest incomplete disposition. Accessible research tables must keep claim labels, missing controls and rejection reasons visible beside findings rather than using color or confident prose alone.
