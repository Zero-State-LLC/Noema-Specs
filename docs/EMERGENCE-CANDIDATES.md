# Emergence Candidates (v0.3)

Conservative candidate label — **not** proof of metaphysical novelty.

## Indicative combination (not all required)

* behavior not explicitly scripted by world rules  
* not directly instructed by situation text  
* novel combination of known actions  
* persistent structured strategy  
* multi-agent structure not preauthored  
* external artifact/institution creation  
* unexpected transfer or adaptation  

Record what was **not specified**. Claim label typically `SPECULATIVE` until Lab controls.
