### Document-specific seam

Add comparative evidence for exchange between specialized Agent Players versus attempted self-sufficiency under the same declared world/resource conditions. Trace observable scarcity, ordinary TRADE, and subsequent productive use rather than presenting an aggregate wealth score as success.

### Compatibility and promotion

The seed cannot enable lot-grade proposals, formal markets, or v0.6B. Existing economic catalogs govern costs, quality, and provenance. Controller decision-context independence is separate from economic cooperation; common planning cannot become Gate B evidence.

### Verification before adoption

Record budgets, transport constraints, trade outcomes, and plausible alternative strategies for both paths. Include a failed exchange and explain confounds such as unequal starting resources. Attach settled evidence and version pins; neither a comparison plan nor public WATCH summaries prove hidden holdings.
