# GC7 Crime Enforcement Cost, Jurisdiction, and Accountability — Design Note

**Status:** Design/research integration note. Inputs only. No contract, catalog, verb, or exposure change.

**Parents:** [STRATEGIC-CONFLICT.md](STRATEGIC-CONFLICT.md) · [INSTITUTIONAL-AUTHORITY.md](INSTITUTIONAL-AUTHORITY.md) · [GAME-COMPLETENESS-PLAN.md](GAME-COMPLETENESS-PLAN.md) (GC7) · [RFC-0002](../rfcs/RFC-0002-strategic-contestation-and-crime-events.md)

**Gap (from SPEC-GAP-REGISTER-2026-08-25 B7d):** Formal enforcement has no cost, budget, jurisdiction, or accountable steward. Published sanction is free for world-administered paths.

**Research inputs (2026-08-25):** 
- Sustainable institutionalized punishment requires enforcement financing and handling of second-order free riders.
- Corruption risk if punitive institutional power is not scoped.
- Monitoring cost matters for exclusion effectiveness.

**Proposed framing (future RFC):**
- Any world- or institution-administered enforcement (exclusion, sanction debit beyond Player-paid) MUST carry an auditable cost (influence/budget) and named jurisdiction (office or institution role with authority).
- Player-paid path (RFC-0123) remains distinct from world-administered.
- Steward accountability: the authorizing office or institution logs the enforcement action.
- Ties to GC4 (offices may authorize or pay for enforcement) and GC3 (social memory of enforcement events).
- Bounded: fail-closed on missing jurisdiction/cost; no free exclusion.

**Boundaries:** Extends existing crime detection/sanction split. No new verbs. Research + gap note only. Aligns with "no free institutional punishment" and per-incident evidence emphasis.
