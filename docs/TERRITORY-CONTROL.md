# Territory and Control

## Principle

Control is **emergent**, not a paint-the-map flag.

Control of a site or route arises from the combination of:

```text
presence
+ infrastructure ownership / control
+ access rights
+ organization authority
```

## Establishing control

An actor or organization gains practical control when:

1. It maintains sustained presence (agents or infrastructure) in the site, **and**
2. It owns or primarily operates key infrastructure in that site, **and/or**
3. Formal access agreements or organizational policy restrict others.

## What control grants

- Preferential production and storage benefits from local infrastructure
- Ability to set or influence local access policies (later milestone)
- Stronger position in contested resolution
- Visibility in Realm and World Reports
- Historical claim weight

## What control does **not** grant

- Absolute ownership of all resources in the room
- Immunity to sabotage, degradation, or crime
- Automatic success in combat or negotiation
- Forced exclusion of other agents without further mechanics

## Contested territory

A site becomes contested when two or more actors maintain competing presence or infrastructure claims. Contested status is visible in observations and reports. Resolution occurs through:

- Economic pressure
- Infrastructure condition races
- Negotiation / agreements
- Strategic conflict actions ([STRATEGIC-CONFLICT.md](STRATEGIC-CONFLICT.md); full resolution rules are a next game milestone and may require RFC for new events)

## Neutral territory

Default state. Open movement and harvest under normal rules. No single controller.

## Transitions

Control is never instantaneous. It accumulates through actions and infrastructure investment and can erode through neglect, sabotage, organization fracture, or superior competing investment.

## Canonical state

No separate “territory paint” table is required for v0.1. Control is derived from Entity ownership/controller fields, Organization membership, and sustained presence recorded in the ledger and world state ([DATA-MODEL.md](DATA-MODEL.md)).
