## Extension Points

Non-normative report-design guidance; new report families remain candidates, not enabled public projections.

### Public consequence comparison seam

Develop side-by-side examples for a construction scar, an institutional change, or a public breach: eligible canonical source, permitted report line, and the information deliberately omitted. Reuse the owning World Report or WATCH projection instead of creating a parallel news/event stream; an Admin Operator Digest is not a source of public authority.

### Retained invariants

Reports remain deterministic, permission-filtered derivatives rather than events or world writes. Preserve per-slice cadence, retention, silence, and partial-observability rules; a WR-S0 report does not become a WATCH line just because other slices support public output. Exclude private relationship edges, hidden locations, motives, detector confidence, and research-only identifiers. Humans watch through platform roles, not Player identity.

### Compatibility and promotion gate

Map each example to its Accepted WR/WATCH slice before treating the historical gap statement as new work. Changed families, fields, or exposure require the owning contract decision and fixtures, not merely this seed. [RFC-0129](../rfcs/RFC-0129-crime-detected-payload-reconciliation.md) accepts optional crime victim/visibility fields without a visibility default; public `visibility` and `PUBLIC_HISTORY` must be co-extensive as specified there. That acceptance neither deploys a crime producer nor makes every crime or named victim public.

### Concrete review checks

Compare identical eligible inputs for deterministic report selection, then replace one source with a private event and require omission without a revealing placeholder. For crime candidates, exercise absent visibility, PARTIES visibility, the valid public flag/visibility pair, and inconsistent pairs rejected under the owning contract. Check that missing `victim_id` does not invent a dyadic victim. Compare any proposed visual card with its text counterpart and verify no additional facts appear. Run affected report/projection fixtures and Specs validation before promotion; visual, accessibility, and runtime checks still need separate observed evidence.
