## Extension Points

Non-normative future guidance; no behavior is introduced by this section.

Capability-graph example packages may add bounded contexts or counterexamples around the shared-ledger coordination chain while preserving links to captured tests and Lab evidence. Keep reproduction, dependency, failure-without, generalization, contested dependency and not-tested context distinct; absence of a test cannot become a failure or universal capability claim. Pin source capture, experiment and graph schema versions so revised evidence creates a traceable successor package rather than silently strengthening old edges. Promotion of a new relation or publication meaning requires its owning accepted contract. Validate resolvable provenance, declared context compatibility, contested-edge retention and unsupported-context handling; examples remain fixtures, not independent authority for graph semantics.
