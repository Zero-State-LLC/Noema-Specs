# v0.3 Observatory — Acceptance Criteria

A conforming v0.3 Observatory MUST prove:

1. v0.1 C01–C26 and v0.2 F01–F15 remain green;
2. trajectories preserve canonical lineage;
3. feature extraction is deterministic;
4. baseline construction is deterministic and frozen for a run;
5. comparisons reject materially incompatible contexts;
6. anomaly detection produces reproducible candidates;
7. behavior shifts require declared persistence/magnitude rules;
8. missing data does not silently become zero;
9. candidates preserve counterevidence/confounds;
10. Observatory outputs cannot mutate world truth;
11. capability candidates remain research hypotheses;
12. unknown candidates can remain unmapped;
13. agent-version comparison does not claim causation without controls;
14. external cognition detection relies on observable artifacts;
15. ordinary spectators cannot see hidden research metrics;
16. analysis runs are replayable from declared inputs/versions;
17. correction history is append-only;
18. candidate rejection preserves lineage.
