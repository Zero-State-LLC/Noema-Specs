# v0.3 Observatory — Non-goals

Out of scope for v0.3:

* Experiment Lab / perturbation / lesion / ablation / counterfactuals;
* Phenomenon Compiler;
* full Capability Graph;
* Deep Time expansion beyond existing game features;
* scalar intelligence or consciousness scores;
* opaque ML as claim-bearing anomaly authority;
* situation injection (Frontier’s job);
* world-native rewards from research scores.

v0.3 stops at **candidate discovery and triage**.

## Extension Points

Non-normative future guidance; no behavior is introduced by this section.

Future roadmap notes may map an excluded capability to its separately owned release/RFC while preserving v0.3 as candidate discovery and triage. Such pointers do not activate Lab interventions, Compiler capture, Capability Graph publication or Deep Time expansion in Observatory. A proposed boundary change needs explicit acceptance, versioned interfaces and migration implications for v0.3 consumers. Validate that candidate payloads remain hypotheses with provenance, that situation injection remains Frontier-owned, and that no intelligence/consciousness scalar, opaque anomaly authority or world-native research reward enters the v0.3 contract. Keep out-of-scope failures explicit rather than returning invented successful analysis.
