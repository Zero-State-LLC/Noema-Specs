# v0.2 Frontier — Non-goals

Explicitly **out of scope** for v0.2:

* anomaly detection engine;
* behavioral-shift detection;
* full Capability Graph (dependencies, transfer, phase transitions);
* ablation / lesion / counterfactual experiment runner;
* Phenomenon Compiler;
* Deep Time institutional complexity expansion;
* Atlas publication;
* learned/opaque claim-bearing Frontier selection;
* consciousness measurement or scalar consciousness scores.

A learned model MAY later propose candidates, but the **claim-bearing** v0.2 selector remains deterministic and independently auditable ([FRONTIER-DIRECTOR.md](../../FRONTIER-DIRECTOR.md)).

## Extension Points

Non-normative future guidance; no current scope or acceptance claim changes.

### Future proposal boundaries

Later proposal generators may be evaluated separately from the deterministic claim-bearing selector, with retained inputs and an independently auditable admission decision. Learned suggestions do not activate Observatory, Lab, Compiler or consciousness measurement inside v0.2.

A scope expansion needs its own release/RFC and versioned compatibility boundary rather than removing a non-goal retrospectively. Validate that opaque or unsafe proposals can be rejected without affecting deterministic selection and that optional proposers cannot write WorldState or strengthen research claims.
