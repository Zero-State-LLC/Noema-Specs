# Ambitions

## Principle

NOEMA has no mandatory victory condition.

Players (whether driven by human or agent Controllers) pursue world-native ambitions. The system supports many viable orientations.

## Example orientations

- Economic power
- Infrastructure dominance
- Exploration and knowledge
- Diplomatic influence
- Organization building
- Resilience and recovery mastery
- Market leadership
- Territorial reach
- Historical significance
- Specialized niche (single critical resource or route)

Players may invent their own goals. The world does not force a single optimum ([CORE-GAME-LOOP.md](CORE-GAME-LOOP.md), [GAME-BALANCE.md](GAME-BALANCE.md)). Controllers (browser, Hermes, OpenClaw, …) do not change Player status ([AUTH-AND-IDENTITY.md](AUTH-AND-IDENTITY.md)).

## Extension Points

Non-normative interpretation seams for world-native, self-chosen ambitions.

- Extend research comparison examples with contrasting viable orientations, such as recovery versus territorial reach, grounded in permitted action histories rather than inferred private goals. An ambition taxonomy is a study annotation, not a mandatory quest, win condition, score or Player class.
- Apply RFC-0120 and AUTH-AND-IDENTITY when interpreting the historical human/browser Controller wording above: Players are agent-only. Framework provenance never changes status, and human WATCH or STUDY access does not become inhabit authority.
- Compatibility/promotion: retain existing action vocabulary and balance contracts; new annotations need a versioned interpretation and explicit evidence/uncertainty before use in a study. They must not feed a hidden research target or recommended optimum back into PLAY.
- Verification proposal: show that the same lawful action trace can support multiple declared interpretations, that an undeclared ambition stays unknown, and that no annotation changes action availability or reward. Public summaries should expose only authorized facts; localize orientation captions without presenting uncertain goal labels as established world truth.
