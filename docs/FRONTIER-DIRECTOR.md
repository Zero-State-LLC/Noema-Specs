# Frontier Director

## Purpose

The Frontier Director ranks and schedules discoverable opportunities so the player encounters a legible, varied, and personally meaningful frontier. It adapts the route, never the truth.

## Inputs

- reachable capabilities and unmet graph requirements;
- evidence coverage, contradictions, and replication gaps;
- player-selected interests and deferred threads;
- recent challenge dimensions, outcomes, fatigue signals, and accessibility settings;
- eligible compiled phenomena, prerequisites, costs, hazards, and cooldowns.

The Director MUST NOT read unrevealed truth merely to judge whether the player's hypothesis is correct.

## Candidate pipeline

1. Filter for release eligibility, reachability, safety, compatibility, and hard preferences.
2. Reject dead ends lacking a viable evidence and recovery path.
3. Score remaining candidates.
4. Apply diversity and repetition constraints.
5. Select from the top bounded set using a named deterministic random stream.
6. Emit the chosen opportunity plus an explanation safe for the player.

A reference score may combine learning value, unresolved relevance, capability utility, novelty, interest affinity, pacing fit, and recovery value, minus cost, fatigue, repetition, and risk. Weights and features MUST be versioned.

## Scaffolding

The Director MAY schedule calibration tasks, comparison sites, resource relief, mentor prompts, or alternative instruments. It MUST NOT alter observed history, lower evidence validity after the fact, secretly waive capability requirements, or rewrite a phenomenon law.

## Player agency

The player can inspect why an opportunity is suggested, pin a thread, decline without punishment, and choose among materially different candidates. Declines inform repetition control but do not count as epistemic failure.

## Cold start and degradation

Cold start uses authored introductory arcs and declared preferences. With incomplete analytics, the Director falls back to deterministic content metadata. If ranking fails, it returns a safe reachable shortlist rather than blocking progression.

## Fairness and privacy

Sensitive traits and inferred protected characteristics MUST NOT drive difficulty or content. Behavioral adaptation is local or consented, inspectable, resettable, and deletable. Accessibility constraints are hard filters, not negative scoring signals.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every recommendation references an eligible reachable artifact.
- Identical inputs and policy version produce identical ranked output.
- Explanations cite visible goals without leaking hidden truth.
- Repeated failure increases support options without changing laws.
- A player can always decline and see at least one alternative when inventory permits.
