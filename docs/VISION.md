# Vision

## One sentence

Noema is a discovery game in which understanding a coherent alien reality is the primary form of progression.

## Player fantasy

You are not the chosen hero receiving answers. You are a field researcher at the edge of the known, building instruments, noticing anomalies, arguing with your own assumptions, and earning the ability to act on what you can demonstrate.

## Experience pillars

### Epistemic agency

The player chooses what to measure, what to hold constant, what would change their mind, and when evidence is strong enough to act. The game rewards model quality rather than keyword matching.

### Coherent mystery

Phenomena emerge from stable composable rules. Surprise comes from interaction and incomplete access, not arbitrary exceptions. The product may conceal causes, never fabricate hindsight.

### Instruments as verbs

Tools change what can be perceived and manipulated. A new instrument is both a capability and a new vocabulary for asking the world questions.

### Productive failure

A disproven hypothesis, contaminated trial, or null result changes the evidence landscape. Recovery is possible, and the system explains what is known without revealing the answer.

### Living frontier

The next challenge responds to demonstrated knowledge, unresolved contradictions, chosen interests, and pacing. Adaptation selects encounters and support. It does not alter physical truth.

## Audience

Noema is for players who enjoy systemic exploration, inference, construction, simulation, and the pleasure of “I figured it out.” It must remain approachable to players without formal scientific training through layered tools, plain language, visual reasoning, and optional depth.

## Desired emotions

Wonder, curiosity, disciplined doubt, competence, surprise, and earned confidence. Avoid helpless obscurity, homework-like grading, false authority, and grind.

## Success measures

- Players can explain a discovery in their own words and cite the evidence that changed their mind.
- Multiple valid experimental paths reach the same underlying rule.
- Failed trials frequently lead to a meaningful next action.
- Unlocks feel causally connected to demonstrated understanding.
- Replays and shared investigations produce comparable results under comparable conditions.

## Boundaries

Noema is not a real-world laboratory credential, a substitute for scientific education, or a claim that all inquiry follows one linear method. The setting may use fictional science, but the reasoning loop should model intellectual honesty: uncertainty, provenance, falsifiability, replication, and revision.

## North-star scenario

A player encounters a luminous organism that retreats from one scanner but not another. They notice the scanners differ in pulse interval, propose that rhythmic energy rather than brightness causes the response, preregister predictions, build a controlled trial, and reproduce the effect at a second site. That result unlocks a low-impact signaling instrument, which reveals a new ecosystem relationship and opens the next frontier. The system never says “correct because the designer intended it.” It shows observations, competing explanations, uncertainty, and why the capability requirement was satisfied.
