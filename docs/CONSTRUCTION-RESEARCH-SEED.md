**Gap statement:** GC2-S0 pins construction on existing infrastructure using current events (CONSTRUCT/DISMANTLE). The full generalized construction model (distinct from REPAIR/INFRASTRUCTURE, with broader scope, lineage writing, and world modification) remains the open ABSENT per GAME-COMPLETENESS-PLAN. BUILD family mechanics stay deferred; the campaign requires a complete authority that couples to geography, resources, Deep Time, and other GCs without creating an industry.

## Proposed research framing for future authority work

Inherit all constraints from GAME-COMPLETENESS-PLAN GC2 and existing CONSTRUCTION.md / GC2-FIRST-SLICE.md.

Candidate extensions (to be settled later):
- Generalized BUILD family (distinct from REPAIR): CONSTRUCT, DISMANTLE, UPGRADE, REPURPOSE, etc., with closed types and dependency tracking.
- Lineage and attribution into Deep Time (scars, artifacts, names, historical reconstruction).
- Coupling to GEOGRAPHY, RESOURCE-ECONOMY, and pressures (scarcity, distance, dependency, uncertainty).
- Observable projections for WATCH/spectators (public construction events without leaking private intent).
- Integration with other GCs (e.g., specialization affecting construction efficiency, social memory recording shared builds) without duplication.
- Procedural/verifiable generation rules that produce persistent player-created structure.

Cross-cutting (inherit):
- Complexity doctrine A–J (model causes, not industries; no crafting trees or level discounts in base model).
- Research sits outside the five nested game loops.
- Existing S0 pins (reuse ENTITY_* events; no new verbs for first cuts).
- Deep Time lineage for all persistent modifications.
- Partial observability and fail-closed rules.
