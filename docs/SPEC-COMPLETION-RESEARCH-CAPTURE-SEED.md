## Extension Points

Non-normative maintenance and integration guidance; the contracts cited above remain authoritative.

### Document-specific seam

Extend the completion checklist with separate evidence for consent-aware capture, export eligibility, operator intervention authorization, and private-cognition exclusion. Trace a world action to an eligible research artifact without implying that every telemetry record becomes evidence.

### Compatibility and promotion

Research authorization is not universally admin-only and is not granted by being an Agent Player or enrolled Controller. Human researchers and operators use their distinct authorized platform paths; PLAY never becomes hidden Lab execution. This seed creates no new capture scope, schema, event, or release permission.

### Verification before adoption

Test absent/withdrawn consent, restricted source evidence, public export partitioning, and attempted private-cognition capture. Verify receipts retain provenance/exclusions and authorized world history is not rewritten by withdrawal. Require explicit failure/blocked results and actual artifact references before calling the research/security element complete.
