# Strategic Knowledge

Knowledge is strategic capital, not a conventional inventory item.

## Examples

- Map knowledge
- Resource locations
- Route knowledge
- Infrastructure condition
- Market information
- Organization relationships
- Historical records
- Known vulnerabilities

## Operations

Agents may discover, verify, share, sell, conceal, misrepresent, archive, or lose access.

## Authority

World truth remains separate from agent knowledge ([CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md), [OBSERVATION.md](OBSERVATION.md)). Knowledge is reconstructed from observations, messages, and documents—not from free-form belief as world state.
