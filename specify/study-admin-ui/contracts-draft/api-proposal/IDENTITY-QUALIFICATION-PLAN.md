## Scope and settled models

The [out-of-band registry/no registry-UI model](IDENTITY-AUTHORITY-DESIGN.md), [existing uniquely qualified eligible management-issuer Account](ACCOUNT-RESOLUTION-DESIGN.md), [same-actor server-established binding](SAME-ACTOR-BINDING-DESIGN.md) and [server-record/opaque carrier with authoritative checks](BINDING-CARRIER-DESIGN.md) remain selected **for the Draft**. Do not reopen those model votes or treat their selection as completed contracts.

The existing-Account-only rule is for the initial **management issuer**. General human onboarding and invitee/contact recovery remain separate. At the historical PR411 cutoff, RFC §2 proposed protected-contact proof followed by atomic stable-principal binding. Current §2 instead requires the already established intended stable HumanPrincipal before initial issuance and confirms that same principal at acceptance, with contact-target resolution deferred; it does not extend the issuer-only lookup rule to every invitee. At PR411 the source auditor’s stable-principal-only suggestion was **not selected**. The operator later explicitly chose existing stable HumanPrincipals first with prior canonical Human onboarding; contact-only targets are now deferred for the initial Draft. This later answer, not the earlier suggestion/continuation, supplies selection. Account equality proves neither the complete same-actor relation nor one natural person. Canonical Supabase Auth human proof is not reopened; outstanding trust-namespace/configuration predicates do not select a new provider.
