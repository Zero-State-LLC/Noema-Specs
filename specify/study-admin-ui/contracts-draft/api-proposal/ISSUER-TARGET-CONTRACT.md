**Status: operator-selected Draft logical contract; unaccepted, unregistered and unimplemented.** After PR411, the operator separately answered both structured questions: derive the binding from **one fresh existing Admin-authentication event, with no extra global Admin grant**; target **existing stable HumanPrincipals first, with prior canonical Human onboarding and contact-only invitations deferred**. ## Logical producer → binding → consumer

This sequence describes proposed server obligations, not installed fields/endpoints or an assertion that current code supplies the witnesses.

1. **Existing Admin authentication:** the ordinary existing Admin boundary MUST establish valid Admin admission under its independently applicable policy. A study role, generic Human roles.admin text, client-chosen identity, shared operator label or mailbox hash MUST NOT substitute. The derivative MUST NOT grant a new Admin role, add an Admin allowlist member, trigger an additional global Admin credential mint or convert human identity into Player/Controller authority. Independently requested legitimate normal Admin authentication retains its existing semantics.
2. **Same-event provenance:** the controlled producer MUST retain qualified provider/issuer/audience/subject, authentication-method/context and admission provenance from the SAME fresh verified event that established that Admin admission. It MUST demonstrate their relation through trusted server-derived evidence, not pair an arbitrary previously signed Admin token with another Human credential. A session identifier, email equality, matching Account ID or adjacent requests MUST NOT establish this relation. The exact proof format and finite freshness policy remain reviewed contract gates; absent or unqualified required proof MUST refuse the derivative.
3. **Issuer Account:** the producer MUST resolve exactly one current eligible, already-established Noema Account link for that verified acting principal. Missing, ambiguous, stale, ineligible or unavailable linkage MUST refuse; the derivative MUST NOT create/relink an Account, pick a first-row default, invoke combined onboarding, mint Player/Controller credentials or seed registry authority. Matching Account IDs MUST NOT erase verified acting-principal context for multiple operators.
4. **Bound state:** only after those predicates succeed MAY the producer establish the selected authoritative server-side binding and make its opaque management reference usable. The usable state MUST preserve qualified same-event actor/Account/Admin provenance under reviewed validity, revocation, secret storage/presentation and durable/recovery rules. This is not selection of any new record field or a cryptographic protocol.
5. **Management request:** the actual protected consumer MUST resolve and revalidate the authoritative binding, verified acting context, current Account link and valid Admin admission under the qualified presentation protocol, PLUS independently current exact study/world/operation authorization. It MUST refuse missing/stale/revoked/ambiguous/unavailable state, a positive stale cache, ordinary membership or an unbound/shared fallback. A binding is not registry authority or permission to manage every study.
6. **Transition and output:** permitted work MUST retain the real attributable actor and exact authorization through the separately qualified mutation/audit/output commitment boundary. Another read alone MUST NOT be described as a linearization proof. Missing durable acknowledgement or an ambiguous outcome MUST NOT be reported as a successful authority change; replay/recovery MUST NOT swap actors, widen or reactivate terminal authority.

Normal Admin authentication and the management derivative have **separate outcomes**. A legitimate legacy login MAY succeed while the derivative refuses incomplete Account/provenance/currentness; that MUST NOT remove legacy Admin power or imply management readiness. A request for legacy authentication alone MUST NOT silently create a management binding or registry grant. Old shared/mailbox sessions MUST NOT be retroactively relabelled attributable. No second-Admin requirement is introduced.

## Initial invitation targets and acceptance

- Creation/reissue/designation for this initial boundary MUST target an already established stable HumanPrincipal through a qualified server-side identity/namespace/resolution rule. Prior canonical Human onboarding MUST be separate and complete before that identity is targeted; the study invitation MUST NOT provision or relink an Account, Player, Controller or PlayerSession. Human onboarding is not implemented or authorized by this contract.
- A bare email/contact-only target, pending-contact placeholder or unresolved/ambiguous identity MUST refuse initial invitation issuance without sending, minting an invitation credential or creating invitation/grant authority. A permitted existing-identity selector/search, if later specified, MUST resolve an already established principal under reviewed disclosure policy; it MUST NOT silently become contact-target onboarding.
- The management issuer's existing-Account eligibility/Admin predicates MUST NOT be imposed on the invitee. The invitee MUST instead prove the exact intended existing HumanPrincipal under its own qualified identity rules and satisfy current invitation, immutable scope/capability, issuer-authority, finite validity, replay/one-use and durable predicates. A valid ordinary HumanPrincipal does not need management authority to accept its invitation.
- Acceptance MUST create at most one intended scope-limited read-only grant and no broader power. Contact change, account recovery or a new claimant of the same mailbox MUST NOT transfer the accepted grant to a different principal. Stable identity/provider recovery and currentness still require qualification even though contact-target onboarding is deferred.
- Protected verified-contact targeting and its normalization/reassignment/pending-resolution flow remain **deferred and unavailable in this initial scope**, not solved or grandfathered into acceptance. Any future enablement MUST first specify/review its proof and recovery policy, atomic exactly-one stable-principal binding, privacy and lifecycle contracts. No actual legacy data migration or existing contact invitation is asserted.
