# RFC-PROPOSAL: GC2 Owner vs Steward Split (B2b)

**Status:** Minimal draft proposal. Bounded. Design/research only. No contract, catalog, verb, or exposure change.

**Parent gap:** SPEC-GAP-REGISTER-2026-08-25 B2b (OPEN_SPEC in GC2).

**Source:** GC2-OWNER-STEWARD-SEED.md

**Proposed:**
- Owner = canonical creator/attributor (Deep Time lineage, persistent attribution).
- Steward = current responsible party (transferable via succession, office, agreement, or institutional action).
- Default remains owner == steward (existing S0 behavior).
- Enables GC4-style institutional control / bounded authority without losing historical attribution or creating implicit privilege.
- Transfer/recovery semantics governed (narrow RFC scope).

**Scope / Boundaries:** Extends CONSTRUCTION.md + INSTITUTIONAL-AUTHORITY.md. No personhood transfer. No hidden privilege. Research input only. Smallest viable unit for B2b.
