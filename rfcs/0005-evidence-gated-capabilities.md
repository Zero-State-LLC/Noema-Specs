# RFC 0005: Evidence-Gated Capabilities

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-09

## Context

Traditional experience points disconnect progression from Noema's promise that understanding expands agency. Conversely, requiring one designer-intended answer would punish legitimate methods.

## Decision

Action authority is granted through a versioned Capability Graph. Requirements compose prerequisite capabilities, resources, trust, safety, and evidence predicates. Evidence predicates may accept multiple methodological paths and always produce cited, player-safe explanations. Opaque playtime or XP cannot substitute for required evidence.

Evaluation is deterministic and idempotent. Historical valid grants are grandfathered by default. Safety or integrity suspension preserves evidence and emits a reason. Graph releases include reachability, cycle, bottleneck, and migration analysis.

## Consequences

Content design must provide viable evidence paths and robust predicates. Players gain progression that feels earned and inspectable. Migration is more complex than incrementing a level.

## Alternatives

XP levels were rejected as the primary gate. Exact-answer matching was rejected because language and method vary. Fully manual designer grants were rejected because they do not scale or replay consistently.

## Validation

Graph fixtures cover alternative evidence paths, near misses, invalidation, idempotent retry, suspension, grandfathering, and supported version migration.
