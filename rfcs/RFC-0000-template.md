# RFC 0000: Title

## Problem

What issue requires a contract change?

## Context

What existing NOEMA contracts, versions, schemas, protocols, research files, or security boundaries are affected?

## Proposed change

Describe the exact normative change.

## Alternatives

List rejected options and why they were rejected.

## Compatibility

Describe backward compatibility, version negotiation, and migration requirements.

## Data impact

Describe new, changed, migrated, retained, or deleted data.

## Research impact

Describe effects on reproducibility, claim labels, controls, metrics, Atlas releases, or longitudinal comparability.

## Security impact

Describe effects on containment, credentials, public/private partitions, tool access, and abuse resistance.

## Migration

Describe steps for existing worlds, ledgers, bundles, schemas, and implementation repositories.

## Validation

Describe required tests, replay evidence, schema validation, and review artifacts.

## Rollback

Describe how to reverse or supersede the change safely.
