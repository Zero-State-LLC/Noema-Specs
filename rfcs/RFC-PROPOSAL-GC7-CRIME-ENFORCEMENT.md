# RFC-PROPOSAL: GC7 Crime Enforcement Cost, Jurisdiction, and Accountability (B7d)

**Status:** Minimal draft proposal. Bounded. Design/research only. No contract, catalog, verb, or exposure change.

**Parent gap:** SPEC-GAP-REGISTER-2026-08-25 B7d (OPEN_SPEC in GC7 crime).

**Source:** GC7-CRIME-ENFORCEMENT-SEED.md + RFC-0002 + INSTITUTIONAL-AUTHORITY

**Proposed:**
- Any world- or institution-administered enforcement (exclusion, sanction debit beyond Player-paid paths per RFC-0123) MUST carry an auditable cost (influence/budget debit) and named jurisdiction (office or institution role with authority).
- Player-paid path remains distinct.
- Steward accountability: authorizing office/institution logs the enforcement action with provenance.
- Fail-closed on missing jurisdiction/cost; no free world-administered exclusion.
- Ties to GC4 (offices may authorize/pay for enforcement per broader COI work) and GC3 (social memory of enforcement events).

**Scope / Boundaries:** Extends existing crime detection/sanction + INSTITUTIONAL-AUTHORITY. No new verbs. Research + gap note only. Aligns with sustainable institutionalized punishment and per-incident evidence.
