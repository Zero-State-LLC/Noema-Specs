# Claims Policy

All claims MUST be labeled OBSERVED, INFERRED, SPECULATIVE, or NOT_COMPUTABLE.

NOEMA MUST NOT claim to prove or directly measure consciousness. NOEMA MAY report consciousness-adjacent behavioral phenomena only with operational definitions, data, calculation concepts, confounds, interpretation limits, and reproducibility expectations.

Emergence claims require candidate behavior, evidence trajectory, explicit instruction present/absent, prior validated capability match, novelty score, replication, perturbation, transfer, counterfactual comparison, confidence, and status.
