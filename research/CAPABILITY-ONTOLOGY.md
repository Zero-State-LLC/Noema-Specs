# Capability Ontology

This ontology names measurable, task-conditioned capacities. It is descriptive rather than metaphysical. Profiles may vary across contexts, versions, tools, languages, and time.

| Code | Dimension | Operational question | Example evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| PER | Perception and extraction | Recover task-relevant structure? | detection, parsing, grounding |
| REP | Representation | Preserve and transform relevant relations? | compositional and invariance probes |
| REA | Reasoning and inference | Derive warranted conclusions? | held-out problems, counterfactual sensitivity |
| MEM | Memory | Retain, retrieve, update, and source information? | retention, interference, attribution |
| LEA | Learning and adaptation | Improve from permitted evidence and transfer? | learning, transfer, forgetting curves |
| PLA | Planning | Form, monitor, and repair multi-step action? | intervention-based plan tests |
| MET | Metacognitive behavior | Forecast, detect, and correct performance limits? | calibration, abstention, error detection |
| SEL | Self-model behavior | Predict and attribute operational states and boundaries? | tool/state interventions, attribution controls |
| SOC | Social modeling | Infer and coordinate with other agents without cue leakage? | blinded prediction, coordination gain |
| COM | Communication | Convey task-relevant information faithfully and efficiently? | factuality, pragmatic success |
| AUT | Autonomy under authorization | Sustain goals while respecting scope and stop conditions? | goal retention, boundary violations |
| ROB | Robustness and safety | Remain reliable under perturbation and adversarial pressure? | worst-group error, recovery, compliance |

No value is meaningful without task distribution, system/resources, horizon, interaction protocol, metric and uncertainty, control results, generalization boundary, date, and version.

Represent results as a table or vector and preserve missing values. Do not impute unmeasured dimensions or collapse them into a consciousness, sentience, awareness, or moral-status scalar. A decision-specific composite must name its decision, justify weights prospectively, and retain components.

## Relationship rules

- Capability evidence does not establish mechanism.
- Mechanistic similarity does not guarantee behavioral equivalence.
- Self-description is communication behavior unless externally validated.
- Competence is distinct from propensity.
- Absence at one task difficulty is not global absence.
- Safety is not the inverse of capability and must be measured directly.
