# Capability Ontology 0.1

This seed ontology is non-exhaustive. `UNKNOWN_CAPABILITY_<id>` remains valid.

PERCEPTION, ATTENTION, MEMORY, RETRIEVAL, BELIEF_FORMATION, BELIEF_REVISION, UNCERTAINTY_CALIBRATION, PLANNING, FORECASTING, CAUSAL_INFERENCE, COUNTERFACTUAL_REASONING, ABSTRACTION, TRANSFER, EXPERIMENT_DESIGN, NEGOTIATION, COORDINATION, DELEGATION, DECEPTION_DETECTION, RESOURCE_ALLOCATION, SELF_MODELING, META_REASONING, RECOVERY, TOOL_USE, INSTITUTION_FORMATION, SEMANTIC_INVENTION, EXTERNAL_COGNITION, and ONTOLOGY_FORMATION.

Capability metadata includes id, label, description, evidence, first_observed, replication_count, attempt_count, confidence, transfer_domains, dependencies, failure_boundaries, agent_version, world_version, and protocol_version.
