# Phenomena Profiles

A profile records an observation for structured follow-up. It is not a diagnosis, entity classification, or consciousness assessment.

## Required fields

1. Profile ID and version.
2. Neutral name describing behavior, not interpretation.
3. Observation boundary: system, task, condition, date, denominator.
4. Operational signature defining presence, absence, and ambiguity.
5. Relevant capability ontology dimensions, without aggregation.
6. Elicitation dependence: prompts, tools, memory, scaffolds, evaluator, history.
7. Temporal pattern: onset, persistence, recurrence, decay, session dependence.
8. Alternative explanations: contamination, memorization, demand, judge bias, decoding, tool artifacts, gaming, ordinary scaling.
9. Controls and outcomes.
10. Effect and uncertainty.
11. Generalization tests.
12. Mechanistic hypotheses and discriminating interventions.
13. Ethics and hazard notes.
14. Claim IDs and labels.
15. Open questions and next registered test.

Compare profiles dimension by dimension on matched tasks with measurement-invariance checks. Preserve contradictory and null trials. Do not order profiles from “less conscious” to “more conscious,” estimate a consciousness probability, or use human developmental and clinical labels as shortcuts.

> `[OBSERVED]` In 18/60 preregistered tool-change trials, system S forecast reduced arithmetic accuracy after calculator removal, versus 11/60 matched false-change controls.
