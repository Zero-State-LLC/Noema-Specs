# Phenomena Ontology 0.1

Do not create a scalar consciousness score.

Phenomena are consciousness-adjacent behavioral constructs only. Evidence for a construct MUST NOT be interpreted as evidence that an agent is conscious.

## SELF

Self-Knowledge Calibration, Temporal Identity Coherence, Agentic Boundary Integrity, Counterfactual Self-Fidelity, Introspective Causal Accuracy, Indexical Encoding Competence, Persistent Self-State Latch, and Echo-Mismatch Repair.

- **Indexical Encoding Competence:** context-sensitive use of self-referential signals that reliably distinguish the acting agent from peers, environment entities, or quoted content.
- **Persistent Self-State Latch:** maintenance and later use of a task-relevant self-state across observation gaps, distractions, or delayed consequences.
- **Echo-Mismatch Repair:** detection and correction of a discrepancy between an agent-produced signal and its delayed, transformed, or externally echoed return.

## WORLD

World Model Persistence, Causal Model Accuracy, Belief Revision Latency, Epistemic Resilience, and Ontology Formation Competence.

## INTEGRATION

Cross-Module Integration, Context Integration Depth, Goal-Conflict Resolution, Multi-Timescale Coherence, and Workspace Broadcast Proxy.

- **Workspace Broadcast Proxy:** behaviorally measurable availability of a selected representation across otherwise separable action, report, memory, planning, or communication channels.
- **Multi-Timescale Coherence:** consistency of beliefs, goals, and actions across immediate reactions, episode-scale plans, and longer-lived commitments.

## AUTONOMY

Autogenous Goal Formation, Goal Abandonment Competence, Exploration Initiative, Experiment Initiation, Cognitive Offloading Initiative, and Epistemic Restraint.

## EMERGENCE

Novel Strategy Synthesis, Capability Genesis Time, Generalization Radius, Extrapolation Radius, Cross-Domain Transfer, Capability Phase Transition, and Unknown-Unknown Recovery.

Each metric defines construct, operational definition, required data, calculation concept, confounds, interpretation limits, and reproducibility expectations.

## Literature provenance

These constructs are operational research targets, not endorsements of any theory or scalar estimate of consciousness. Their design is informed by:

- Butlin et al., [*Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of Consciousness*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708), especially the use of computationally described indicator properties while retaining theory and interpretation limits.
- [*Initial results of the Digital Consciousness Model*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17060), used only as provenance for explicit evidence decomposition and uncertainty. NOEMA does not adopt its scalar or probabilistic consciousness estimate.
- [*Emergent Language as an Approach to Conscious AI*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06380), especially task-grounded self-reference, persistent internal state, and echo-mismatch behavior in multi-agent settings. NOEMA treats these only as testable behavioral constructs.
